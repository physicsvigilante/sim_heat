# Six rods with two shared contacts

The left subnetwork is the 2, 3, 5 W/K star in three_terminal/star. The right subnetwork is the 0.6, 1, 1.5 W/K triangle. Join left middle to right warm and left cool to right middle. Both joined pairs become ideal, storage-free junctions. The remaining baths stay at 380 K and 280 K. The two junction temperatures must be solved together because heat can pass between them on both sides.

For a unit external temperature difference, measure the junction rises x and y above the cold bath. Eliminate the central star temperature using its balance. The remaining balances give 37x - 21y = 6 and -21x + 46y = 10, so x = 486/1261 and y = 496/1261. The cold terminal receives x + 1.5y = 1230/1261 W at unit difference. These equations supply an independent hand calculation for this illustrative arrangement. The full Modelica assembly and its same-author checking facts support a separate offline replay.
