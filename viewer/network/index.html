Scope

What this viewer does not do

The read-only presentation supports recorded transient and steady thermal compositions. It does not author or edit models, create temperatures or missing ports, evaluate unseen physics, launch OpenModelica, infer geometry, or establish classroom effectiveness. For a new arrangement, a trusted offline adapter must first prepare and check the native-data bundle.
