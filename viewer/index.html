How to read this experiment

What these curves can—and cannot—tell us

Sign is physical orientation

Early bridge flow is negative because the right body is hotter. Recorded samples bracket a reversal between 1,720 and 1,730 seconds; no exact unsampled crossing time is asserted.

Reservoirs do not equilibrate

Maintained hot and cold boundaries allow ongoing heat transfer. The two bodies approach different steady temperatures, unlike an isolated two-body system.
